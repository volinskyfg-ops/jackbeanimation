01 Our Purpose

The Animation Club is a place where everybody who loves to sit and spend hours drawing frames, wants to learn how to bring a brick to life, or just wants their imagination to explode is welcome.

✏️

Individual Growth

Members complete 4–7 small animation projects on their own throughout the year to hone their skills.

🎥

One Big Film

The whole club collaborates on a high-quality 2-minute animated short over the course of the school year.

🏆

Looking Ahead

If the club continues, we'd love to launch an animation festival honoring legendary Disney animator Ollie Johnston, one of the famous "Nine Old Men."

02 How We'll Get There

1

Weekly Workshops

Skill-building sessions covering core animation principles — timing, spacing, weight, and more.

2

Bi-Weekly Critique Circles

Peer feedback sessions to guide members through their individual projects, for beginners and experienced artists alike.

3

A Real Production Pipeline

Storyboarding, key animation, and sound design roles with clear monthly deadlines to complete our group short film.

4

Hands-On Stop-Motion Labs

Members literally learn how to "bring a brick to life," while studying Ollie Johnston's classic animation principles.

04 Join Paly Animation Club

No experience required — just curiosity and a willingness to create. All Paly students are welcome, whether you're a first-time doodler or already building a portfolio.

  • ✔️ Weekly meetings during club hours
  • ✔️ All skill levels welcome
  • ✔️ Officer elections open to all members each year
  • ✔️ Work toward a real, finished group animated film
