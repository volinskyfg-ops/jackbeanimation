01 Our Purpose

The Animation Club is a place where everybody who loves to sit and spend hours drawing frames, wants to learn how to bring a brick to life, or just wants their imagination to explode is welcome.

Individual Growth

Members complete 4–7 small animation projects on their own throughout the year to hone their skills.

One Big Film

The whole club collaborates on a high-quality 2-minute animated short over the course of the school year.

Looking Ahead

If the club continues, we'd love to launch an annual animation festival showcasing student work from throughout the year.

02 How We'll Get There

1

Bi-Weekly Critique Circles

Peer feedback sessions to guide members through their individual projects, for beginners and experienced artists alike.

2

A Real Production Pipeline

Storyboarding, key animation, and sound design roles with clear monthly deadlines to complete our group short film.

3

Hands-On Stop-Motion Labs

Members literally learn how to "bring a brick to life" through guided stop-motion exercises.
